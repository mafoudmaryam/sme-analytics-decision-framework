## 1. Langer and Pütterich (2024)

**Reference:** Benedict Langer and Felix Pütterich. *Assessing Value Creation of Analytics Projects in SMEs*. Wirtschaftsinformatik 2024 Proceedings, paper 12; 19th International Conference on Wirtschaftsinformatik.

**Sources:** [AIS proceedings record and abstract](https://aisel.aisnet.org/wi2024/12/); [TUM-hosted conference manuscript](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1832502/1832502.pdf).

**Verified contribution:** The AIS abstract describes a synthesis of analytics value creation for SMEs and a proposed scoring approach for project prioritisation. The model considers enabling capabilities, risks, business functions, and firm performance. It distinguishes transactional, transformational, strategic, and informational value.

**Evidence boundary:** Bibliographic information and the contribution above were checked against the AIS record. Indexed opening text of the TUM manuscript describes a literature review supported by expert interviews. The complete manuscript was not retrieved for this documentation pass. The user's initial account identifies further empirical validation as a motivation; the precise limitation and supporting passage must still be checked in the full text before attributing a specific validation-gap claim to the authors.

**Questions for full-text review:** How are criteria and scores defined? How are weights handled? What do the interviews support? What validation has already been performed, and what exactly remains untested?

## 2. Bhardwaj, Behl, and Pereira (2025)

**Reference:** Shikha Bhardwaj, Abhishek Behl, and Vijay Pereira. *Proposing an integrative data-analytics framework for micro, small and medium enterprises: a systematic review substantiated by evidence from two case studies*. Annals of Operations Research, 350, 817–852. [DOI: 10.1007/s10479-023-05186-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10479-023-05186-9).

**Reading status:** Full text not yet read. The following summary is limited to the [publisher's abstract and metadata](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10479-023-05186-9).

**Verified contribution:** The abstract reports a review of 42 studies published during 2010–2021. It groups the synthesis into drivers, inhibitors, analytics adoption, and outcomes, and reports supporting case evidence from IT and manufacturing.

**Publication date:** The publisher lists online publication on 9 February 2023 and the volume publication in 2025. This repository cites the 2025 volume version; these dates do not indicate two separate papers.

**Questions requiring full-text reading:** How were studies selected and assessed? How were cases chosen and analysed? Does the framework directly operationalise project selection? What limitations qualify transfer to other SME contexts?

## Preliminary synthesis and unresolved research direction

The first source is directly relevant to project assessment; the second offers broader adoption and organisational context. This is an initial interpretation, not a complete comparison of their methods. Their existence means that the project must carefully distinguish evaluating or adapting existing frameworks from claiming to invent analytics project selection.

The working focus is ranking sensitivity and transparent decision justification. A broader review must establish what prior studies have already tested, whether this focus offers a useful contribution, and what comparisons are feasible. No claim that the research gap is proven is made here.
