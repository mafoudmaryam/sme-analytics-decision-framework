# Planned literature search

This is a prospective procedure. No comprehensive search, screening count, or completed review is claimed.

## Scope and sources

Search AIS eLibrary, publisher platforms, Google Scholar, and institutional access to Scopus or Web of Science if available. Record the actual sources used and access limitations. Start with English-language work published from 2010 to the search date, include the two starting references, and use backward and forward citation tracing to identify relevant earlier work. Report the language and date restrictions as limitations.

Candidate query groups:

- (SME OR MSME OR "small and medium enterprises") AND ("business analytics" OR "data analytics") AND ("project selection" OR prioritisation OR prioritization OR "value creation")
- (analytics AND "project evaluation") AND (framework OR "multi-criteria" OR scoring)
- ("decision support" AND "project selection") AND (sensitivity OR transparency OR consistency)

Adapt syntax to each database and preserve the exact executed query, date, filters, and result count. The strings above are planned queries, not a record of searches already performed.

## Screening and extraction

Include work with explicit relevance to SME analytics value, project evaluation, or transferable decision-support evaluation methods. Exclude duplicates, unsupported promotional material, and records without sufficient information for the claim being considered. Keep inaccessible but relevant records in a pending-reading list instead of treating abstracts as full-text evidence. Document exclusion reasons.

For each included study, record citation, version, access level, enterprise definition, sector/country, research design, criteria, weights, aggregation, uncertainty handling, comparator, evaluation evidence, limitations, and precise supporting pages or sections. Separate author-reported results from this project's interpretation. Identify adaptations and unresolved questions.

The initial review will be conducted by one researcher; this creates a screening and interpretation limitation. Do not describe it as independently double-screened. Seek feedback where feasible and log subsequent revisions.

## Completion gate

Before implementation, produce an evidence matrix, a justified comparator selection, and a revised statement of contribution. Do not use the label "systematic review" for the finished output unless the executed and documented procedure supports it.
